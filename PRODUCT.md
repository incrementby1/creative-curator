# Product

## Purpose and audience

Creative Curator helps solo brand owners turn scattered ideas into a coherent, defensible Starter Brand Blueprint. Brand Constellation is the primary workspace: a nonlinear source of truth where evidence, assumptions, ideas, decisions, challenges, and outputs can evolve without a prescribed wizard sequence.

MVP serves people building a brand from scratch. Later project branches may support existing-brand work and campaigns by reusing the same graph. Earlier brief → Brand DNA → directions sessions remain owner-scoped, read-only legacy records; they are not converted into graph evidence or relationships.

## Product journey

1. Land, sign up, or sign in.
2. View Projects and create one through a short adaptive diagnostic.
3. Capture and connect user-supplied material in Brand Constellation.
4. Ask Hermes to challenge a selected semantic subgraph.
5. Preview, accept, or reject proposals; resolve, defer, or intentionally override challenges.
6. Approve working decisions and generate an interactive Starter Brand Blueprint.
7. Export a dated, versioned PDF snapshot from the same semantic HTML.
8. Return later to the living graph, saved themes, provider settings, snapshots, and legacy archive.

## Product rules

- Semantic graph, layout, annotations, and media are separate persistence domains. Arrangement, drawing, and decorative media never become evidence or affect readiness.
- Hermes is an active creative director, not an agreeable chatbot. It labels unsupported assumptions, contradictions, cultural or psychological implications, and downstream consequences.
- Hermes receives only selected intent, relevant versioned nodes and edges, compact branch summaries, and applicable unresolved challenges. It never receives the full graph, annotations, or media by default.
- Hermes suggestions remain proposals until explicit acceptance. Acceptance is atomic and version checked; Hermes never silently mutates, approves, moves, or rearranges accepted graph state.
- Foundational changes mark dependent work for review rather than deleting it. Superseded and trashed work remains recoverable through history.
- Quick capture never requires a provider. Provider failure leaves graph editing, drafts, selection, and viewport available.
- Canonical MVP output is an interactive Starter Brand Blueprint. Portable output is a versioned PDF snapshot. Early snapshots disclose unresolved assumptions, challenges, and readiness warnings.

## Personality and principles

Precise, professional, calm, and constructively challenging. Keep work primary, decisions legible, controls familiar, continuity explicit, and system state visible. Earn trust through restraint instead of spectacle.

## Accessibility and resilience

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: complete keyboard operation, structured graph alternative, visible focus, screen-reader announcements, reduced motion, 44-by-44-pixel targets, sufficient contrast in Paper, Graphite, and Project, and no color-only meaning. Mobile supports complete brand development through focused navigation and automatic layout; precision freeform arrangement stays desktop-only.

Pending semantic edits are owner/project scoped, idempotent, ordered, capped at 25 records and 64 KiB UTF-8 payload each, and never store secrets or raw provider material. Replay stops on conflict. Compare is read-only; Keep mine requires confirmation; Accept latest preserves canvas context. Terminal edits can be discarded or held in memory for explicit review and never replay silently.

## Anti-references

- Editorial-magazine staging, oversized serif display, ornamental composition, or content treated as publication.
- Generic AI styling: glassmorphism, bloom, gradient text, glowing surfaces, skewed gradients, or decorative automation diagrams.
- Glossy SaaS patterns, mandatory dark canvas, invented standard controls, hover-only behavior, or decorative perpetual motion.

## Deliberate MVP omissions

Autonomous external research, realtime multi-user collaboration, background-job infrastructure, finished logos and full asset libraries, campaign generation in the graph, public Blueprint sharing, slides, DOCX, editable design-file exports, remote Supabase setup, and a proprietary graph renderer are deferred.
