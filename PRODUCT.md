# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creative Curator serves solo brand owners who need to make creative decisions without an agency-sized process. They use it both for quick campaign direction and ongoing brand management. Their primary task is turning a clear brief into a defensible direction, refining that direction, and producing an artifact without losing context between visits.

## Product Purpose

Creative Curator is a guided creative-review workspace. It helps a brand owner define a brief, inspect an AI-generated brand hypothesis, compare distinct creative directions, reject weak options with structured feedback, approve a refined survivor, and generate a final artifact. Success means the user reaches a confident, explainable decision quickly and can return later with their account and provider settings intact. Saved creative-session recovery is a separate product capability.

## Brand Personality

Precise, professional, and calm. The interface should feel dependable and task-focused, with the earned familiarity and restraint of Notion. It should communicate competence without looking sterile or turning routine work into spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Editorial-magazine styling, including oversized serif headlines, ornamental layouts, or content staged like a publication.
- Generic AI-product styling, including gradient text, glowing surfaces, glass-heavy cards, decorative automation diagrams, and vague assistant copy.
- Glossy SaaS presentation patterns that make configuration and creative work feel like marketing pages.
- Novel controls or motion that make standard workflows harder to understand.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the work primary. Navigation, AI status, and system controls should support the brief-to-output flow rather than compete with it.
2. Make decisions legible. Show what changed, why a direction survived, and what the user can do next.
3. Prefer familiar product patterns. Use clear forms, navigation, settings, and feedback instead of invented interaction models.
4. Preserve continuity deliberately. User settings survive login cycles; drafts and creative sessions state their persistence limits and provide clear recovery paths.
5. Earn trust through restraint. Use direct language, predictable behavior, and visible system state rather than decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus states, semantic structure, screen-reader announcements, reduced motion, sufficient text and control contrast, minimum 44-by-44-pixel touch targets, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
